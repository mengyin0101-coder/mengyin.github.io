01 / Year-round Sports PR

01 / 全年体育公关

HSBC Junior Golf

Year-round PR and event communications for China's official junior golf development system, co-operated by the China Golf Association, HSBC, The R&A and IMG.

围绕中国官方青少年高尔夫人才培养体系的全年公关与赛事传播项目，由中国高尔夫球协会、汇丰银行、The R&A 与 IMG 联合运营。

Overview

Launched in 2007, the program is the only officially recognized full junior golf talent development system in China, connecting education, grassroots participation, graded competitions, national rankings and international opportunities.

汇丰青少年于 2007 年启动，是国内唯一官方认证的完整青少年高尔夫人才培养体系，连接高尔夫普及、基层参与、分级赛事、全国积分排名和国际赛事机会。

My Role

I led the programme PR team, overseeing year-round communications planning, visual direction, social-media optimisation, media and KOL relationships, partner communication, and on-site media-centre operations.

我负责统筹项目公关团队，覆盖全年传播策略、视觉创意策划与把控、社媒优化、媒体 / KOL / 合作伙伴关系维护，以及赛季现场媒体中心管理与媒体服务。

Challenge

The challenge was to sustain attention across a full season while explaining a complex development pathway from grassroots participation to top-tier national junior events and international wild-card opportunities.

传播挑战在于：如何在完整赛季中持续保持关注度，同时讲清楚从校园 / 训练营入门，到全国顶级青少年赛事，再到海外国际赛事外卡机会的完整晋升路径。

Strategy

The strategy positioned the program not as a single tournament series, but as a long-term junior golf pathway connecting official hierarchy, player growth stories, media authority, social engagement and sponsor value.

策略上不把它当成单一赛事系列，而是作为长期青少年高尔夫成长通道来传播。内容连接官方赛事等级、球员成长故事、权威媒体背书、社交互动机制和赞助商品牌价值。

Execution

  • Annual PR review and next-year planning
  • Season launch communication planning
  • On-site media center management and media service
  • Media, KOL and partner relationship maintenance
  • Social media operation and campaign planning
  • 年度 PR 复盘与次年规划
  • 赛季启动传播策划
  • 现场媒体中心管理与媒体服务
  • 媒体、KOL 与合作伙伴关系维护
  • 社交媒体运营与 campaign 策划

Results / Evidence

The 2023 PR review reported more than RMB 78.3 million in estimated media value, 244 invited media outlets and 1,993 published articles. The January-September 2024 review recorded more than RMB 95 million in estimated media value, 206 invited media outlets and 1,538 published articles.

2023 年 PR 复盘包含全年媒体价值超 7,830 万、邀请发稿媒体 244 家、发稿总量 1,993 篇；2024 年 1-9 月复盘包含媒体价值超 9,500 万、邀请发稿媒体 206 家、发稿总量 1,538 篇。

Key Learnings

A long-running development programme needs more than event announcements. Its communications system has to make the pathway visible, prove player progress and give partners a clear reason to stay engaged through the season.

长期人才培养项目不能只发布赛事信息。传播体系需要让成长路径清晰可见、持续呈现球员进阶，并让合作伙伴在整个赛季中持续感受到参与价值。

02 / Nationwide Brand Campaign & Community Activation

02 / 全国品牌 Campaign 与社群激活

Saucony Triumph 24

A nationwide new-product launch campaign that translated Triumph 24's comfort proposition into repeatable Saucony Run Club experiences across six cities, supported by recap analysis and an extended orienteering concept.

一个围绕 Triumph 24 新品上市的全国跑步品牌 campaign，把舒适产品利益转化为六城可复制的 Saucony Run Club 社群体验，并连接活动复盘和延展定向赛策划。

Overview

A nationwide Saucony Run Club activation for urban runners, community members and existing users. The 2026 program delivered eight sessions across Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Nanjing, Hangzhou and Xi'an.

面向城市跑者、跑步社群人群和既有 Saucony Run Club 用户的全国社群活动。2026 年共在北京、上海、深圳、南京、杭州和西安落地 8 场活动。

My Role

I led the campaign framework, client proposal deck, activity mechanism, execution plan, recap analysis and extended SRC orienteering / treasure-hunt concept, excluding detailed on-ground implementation.

我负责 campaign 全案框架、客户提案 deck、活动机制、执行方案、复盘分析和 SRC 定向赛 / 寻宝赛延展策划，不包含具体落地执行。

Challenge

The task was to turn a product-comfort proposition into a reason to join, return and share, while creating a format that could work consistently across different city communities.

挑战在于把产品舒适感转化为跑者愿意报名、复参与分享的理由，同时建立一套可在不同城市社群中稳定复制的活动机制。

Strategy

The strategy used one repeatable morning ritual, localized through city routes and community energy, to connect the product message with participation, social content and new-member conversion.

以统一的清晨仪式为核心，再通过城市路线与社群氛围实现本地化，让产品信息自然连接参与、内容传播和新会员转化。

Key Message

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. connected the idea of getting ahead of the day with morning discipline and product comfort, framing victory as calm control of one's own pace.

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. 连接认知先醒、清晨自律和产品舒适感，把“胜利”转化为一种从容掌控节奏的状态。

Execution

  • “Awake Ahead” morning-run activation with city-landmark checkpoints
  • Polaroid and film-camera capture of participants' awake moments
  • Runner stories, Xiaohongshu (RED) content and UGC planning
  • SRC treasure-hunt and orienteering extension planning
  • “先醒一步”晨跑与城市地标打卡机制
  • 通过拍立得 / 胶片相机记录“先醒瞬间”
  • 跑者故事、小红书内容与 UGC 规划
  • SRC 寻宝 / 定向赛延展策划

Results / Evidence

The 2026 nationwide programme generated 5,486 page views and 582 unique participants, producing 192 new member sign-ups at a 33.0% conversion rate. Social output included 36 Xiaohongshu (RED) posts, 2,394 interactions and seven posts exceeding 100 interactions.

2026 年全国活动累计获得 5,486 PV、582 参与 UV，带来 192 名新增会员，拉新转化率达 33.0%。社媒共发布 36 篇小红书笔记、获得 2,394 次互动，并有 7 篇互动破百内容。

Key Learnings

Running campaigns become stronger when product benefits are translated into a lifestyle proposition, a repeatable city-route mechanism and measurable member conversion.

跑步品牌 campaign 的价值在于把产品利益转化为生活方式主张、可复制的城市路线机制和可衡量的会员转化。

Core Idea

核心创意

The campaign reframed comfort as more than a product benefit: it became the confidence to begin earlier, move with less friction and arrive ahead of the day.

项目不只讲产品舒适，而是把舒适转化为一种状态：更早出发、更轻松行动、更从容地领先一天。

一个仪式，六城共鸣

Morning running, landmark checkpoints and Polaroid “awake moments” created a consistent ritual that could be localized through each city's route, community and social content.

晨跑、地标打卡和拍立得“先醒瞬间”构成统一仪式，再通过每座城市的路线、社群与内容表达完成本地化。

03 / Concept Case Study & Strategic Proposal

03 / 概念案例与战略提案

National Geographic Trail Race

A strategic concept proposal for public-sector stakeholders and potential brand sponsors, connecting destination positioning, a trail-race prototype, an outdoor-festival experience and partnership value.

一个面向政府与潜在品牌赞助商的战略概念提案，连接目的地定位、越野赛事原型、户外节体验与商业合作价值。

Project Context

The proposal explored how National Geographic's brand strength could support a destination-led outdoor event platform, using trail running as the entry point and outdoor-festival experiences as the wider cultural layer.

方案探索如何借助 National Geographic 的品牌资产，打造目的地户外活动 IP，以越野跑作为核心入口，以户外生活节体验作为更广泛的文化层。

Strategic Challenge

The proposal needed to speak to both public-sector destination value and brand-sponsor commercial value, balancing cultural storytelling, event feasibility and measurable impact.

提案需要同时面向政府端目的地价值和品牌赞助商商业价值，平衡文化叙事、赛事可行性和可衡量影响。

Concept Direction

The concept reframed a trail race as a “Festival of Wonder”: an event platform where movement becomes a way to explore nature, understand place and create reasons to return.

项目将越野赛事延展为“惊奇生活节”：让运动成为探索自然、理解目的地，并形成重复到访理由的体验平台。

Program Framework

  • Destination storytelling and public-sector value
  • Trail race model and race categories
  • Outdoor festival and signature experiences
  • Operations, promotion and measurement planning
  • 目的地叙事与政府端价值
  • 越野赛事模型与组别
  • 户外生活节与标志性体验
  • 运营、传播与测量规划

Destination Storytelling Logic

The framework connected landscape assets to event participation, content reach, destination travel and annual renewal, creating a loop rather than a single-event story.

框架把山地资源连接到赛事参与、内容传播、目的地旅游和年度复利，形成可持续循环，而非一次性活动叙事。

My Contribution

I developed the overall project framework, strategic insight, extension logic and measurement approach, including how the proposal could serve both public-sector stakeholders and brand sponsors.

我负责整个项目的框架、洞察、延展逻辑和测算思路，包括如何让方案同时面向政府端和潜在赞助商品牌方成立。

Key Learnings

A destination event proposal becomes more credible when cultural meaning, participant experience, operational feasibility and sponsor value are designed as one system.

一个目的地赛事提案，只有把文化意义、参与者体验、运营可行性和品牌合作价值设计为同一套系统，才真正具备说服力。

04 / Community Trail Series Review

04 / 社群越野跑系列复盘

HOKA Trail Running Series

A three-station H1 2026 review translating participation, content performance, runner feedback and authentic trail conditions into practical community and brand insight.

2026 年上半年三站社群越野跑系列复盘，将参与数据、内容表现、跑者反馈与真实赛道体验转化为可执行的社群与品牌洞察。

Overview

The review covered HOKA's Run Wilder community trail series across Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan, examining how trail running, outdoor lifestyle and belonging can strengthen brand storytelling.

项目复盘 HOKA “一起野外”社群越野跑系列的虞山、莫干山和王位山三站，理解越野跑、户外生活方式和社群归属感如何强化品牌叙事。

My Role

I led the post-event analysis and reporting, bringing together participation data, content performance, creator segmentation, runner feedback and location-specific highlights into a structured evaluation.

我负责 recap 分析总结和复盘，将赛事数据、内容表现、达人类型、跑者反馈和分站亮点整合成结构化的活动后评估。

Challenge

The review needed to move beyond headline attendance and content totals, identifying how three distinct trail environments, creator segments and runner responses shaped community quality and future event decisions.

复盘不止统计报名与内容总量，还需要识别三种不同赛道环境、达人类型和跑者反馈如何共同影响社群质量，并为后续活动决策提供依据。

Review Approach

I compared the three locations through registration, participation, completion, content performance, creator value and runner voice, then translated the findings into clear opportunities for content and community operations.

我从报名、参赛、完赛、内容表现、达人价值和跑者声音六个维度对比三站，并将发现转化为内容与社群运营的后续机会。

Execution

  • Compared Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan participation data
  • Reviewed WeChat and Xiaohongshu (RED) content performance
  • Mapped creator value across high-reach, high-engagement and KOC groups
  • Synthesised runner feedback, real trail conditions and station-specific highlights
  • 对比虞山、莫干山和王位山三站参与数据
  • 复盘微信与小红书内容表现
  • 分析高覆盖、高互动与 KOC 达人价值
  • 整合跑者反馈、真实赛道体验和各站亮点

Results / Evidence

Across the three stations, the series recorded 2,927 registrations, 1,904 participants and 1,720 finishers, for a 90.3% completion rate. Social output included 228 pieces of content, 17,381 interactions and an estimated potential reach of 204.8K.

三站系列累计报名 2,927 人、参赛 1,904 人、完赛 1,720 人，完赛率达 90.3%。社交内容共发布 228 篇，累计互动 17,381 次，潜在覆盖约 20.48 万。

Key Learnings

The strongest community insight comes from connecting event data with the lived reality of the trail. This makes the recap useful not only as a report, but also as a planning tool for the next event cycle.

最有价值的社群洞察，来自数据与真实赛道、真实跑者体验的结合。这样复盘不仅是一份报告，也能成为下一轮活动规划的工作工具。
