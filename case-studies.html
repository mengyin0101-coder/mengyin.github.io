01 / Year-round Sports PR

01 / 全年体育公关

HSBC Junior Golf

Year-round event communications highlighting junior golf development and the value of the competition programme.

通过全年赛事传播，持续呈现青少年高尔夫的发展与赛事价值。

Overview

项目概览

Launched in 2007, the program is the only officially recognized full junior golf talent development system in China, connecting education, grassroots participation, graded competitions, national rankings and international opportunities.

汇丰青少年于 2007 年启动，是国内唯一官方认证的完整青少年高尔夫人才培养体系，覆盖高尔夫普及、基层参与、分级赛事、全国积分排名与国际赛事机会。

My Role

我的角色

I connected annual PR planning, media relations, social content and on-site media-centre operations into a season-long communications programme.

统筹全年公关规划、媒体关系与社媒内容，并负责现场媒体中心管理，串联赛季传播与赛事现场执行。

Challenge

项目挑战

The challenge was to sustain attention across a full season while explaining a complex development pathway from grassroots participation to top-tier national junior events and international wild-card opportunities.

挑战在于如何贯穿整个赛季保持关注度，并清晰讲述从校园与训练营启蒙，到全国顶级赛事、再到国际赛事外卡机会的完整成长路径。

Strategy

传播策略

The strategy positioned the program not as a single tournament series, but as a long-term junior golf pathway connecting official hierarchy, player growth stories, media authority, social engagement and sponsor value.

不把项目当作单一赛事系列传播，而是将其定位为长期的青少年高尔夫成长通道：串联官方赛事体系、球员成长故事、权威媒体背书、社群互动与赞助商价值。

Execution

项目执行

  • Annual PR review and next-year planning
  • Season launch communication planning
  • On-site media center management and media service
  • Media, KOL and partner relationship maintenance
  • Social media operation and campaign planning
  • 年度 PR 复盘与次年规划
  • 赛季启动传播策划
  • 现场媒体中心管理与媒体服务
  • 媒体、KOL 与合作伙伴关系维护
  • 社交媒体运营与 campaign 策划

Results / Evidence

成果与依据

The 2023 PR review reported more than RMB 78.3 million in estimated media value, 244 invited media outlets and 1,993 published articles. The January-September 2024 review recorded more than RMB 95 million in estimated media value, 206 invited media outlets and 1,538 published articles.

2023 年全年媒体价值超过 7,830 万元，邀请发稿媒体 244 家、发稿总量 1,993 篇；2024 年 1 至 9 月媒体价值超过 9,500 万元，邀请发稿媒体 206 家、发稿总量 1,538 篇。

Key Learnings

关键收获

A long-running development programme needs more than event announcements. Its communications system has to make the pathway visible, prove player progress and give partners a clear reason to stay engaged through the season.

长期人才培养项目不能只发布赛事信息。传播体系需要让成长路径清晰可见，持续呈现球员进阶，也让合作伙伴在整个赛季持续感受到参与价值。

02 / Nationwide Brand Campaign & Community Activation

02 / 全国品牌 Campaign 与社群激活

Saucony Triumph 24

Turning a new-product launch into a brand experience for urban runners.

将新品上市转化为城市跑者可参与的品牌体验。

Overview

项目概览

A nationwide Saucony Run Club activation for urban runners, community members and existing users. The 2026 program delivered eight sessions across Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Nanjing, Hangzhou and Xi'an.

面向城市跑者、跑步社群与既有 Saucony Run Club 用户的全国活动。2026 年在北京、上海、深圳、南京、杭州与西安共落地 8 场。

My Role

我的角色

I led campaign planning, translating product comfort into a morning-run concept and city participation mechanics, then developed execution plans and reviewed results.

主导活动全案，将产品舒适感转化为晨跑创意与城市参与机制，完成执行方案及活动复盘。

Challenge

项目挑战

The task was to turn a product-comfort proposition into a reason to join, return and share, while creating a format that could work consistently across different city communities.

挑战在于把产品的舒适感，转化为跑者愿意报名、再次参与并主动分享的理由，同时建立可在不同城市社群中稳定复制的活动机制。

Strategy

活动策略

The strategy used one repeatable morning ritual, localized through city routes and community energy, to connect the product message with participation, social content and new-member conversion.

以统一的“清晨仪式”为核心，再通过城市路线与社群氛围完成在地表达，让产品信息自然连接参与、内容传播与新会员转化。

Key Message

核心信息

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. connected the idea of getting ahead of the day with morning discipline and product comfort, framing victory as calm control of one's own pace.

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. 将“先醒一步”、清晨自律与产品舒适感连接起来，把“胜利”诠释为从容掌控自身节奏的状态。

Execution

项目执行

  • “Awake Ahead” morning-run activation with city-landmark checkpoints
  • Polaroid and film-camera capture of participants' awake moments
  • Runner stories, Xiaohongshu (RED) content and UGC planning
  • SRC treasure-hunt and orienteering extension planning
  • “先醒一步”晨跑与城市地标打卡机制
  • 通过拍立得 / 胶片相机记录“先醒瞬间”
  • 跑者故事、小红书内容与 UGC 规划
  • SRC 寻宝 / 定向赛延展策划

Results / Evidence

成果与依据

The 2026 nationwide programme generated 5,486 page views and 582 unique participants, producing 192 new member sign-ups at a 33.0% conversion rate. Social output included 36 Xiaohongshu (RED) posts, 2,394 interactions and seven posts exceeding 100 interactions.

2026 年全国活动累计获得 5,486 次页面浏览、582 名参与者，带来 192 名新增会员，拉新转化率达 33.0%。社媒发布 36 篇小红书内容，获得 2,394 次互动，其中 7 篇互动量超过 100。

Key Learnings

关键复盘

Running campaigns become stronger when product benefits are translated into a lifestyle proposition, a repeatable city-route mechanism and measurable member conversion.

跑步品牌活动的价值，在于把产品利益转化为生活方式主张、可复制的城市路线机制与可衡量的会员转化。

Core Idea

核心创意

Comfort, Made Into a Morning Advantage

把舒适，变成清晨抢先一步的优势

01

Awake Ahead

先醒一步

The campaign reframed comfort as more than a product benefit: it became the confidence to begin earlier, move with less friction and arrive ahead of the day.

项目不只讲产品舒适，而是把舒适转化为一种状态：更早出发、更轻松行动、更从容地领先一天。

02

One Ritual, Six Cities

一个仪式，六城共鸣

Morning running, landmark checkpoints and Polaroid “awake moments” created a consistent ritual that could be localized through each city's route, community and social content.

晨跑、地标打卡和拍立得“先醒瞬间”构成统一仪式，再通过每座城市的路线、社群与内容表达完成本地化。

03 / Concept Case Study & Strategic Proposal

03 / 概念案例与战略提案

National Geographic Trail Race国家地理 · 越野赛事策划

A destination proposal connecting trail racing, an outdoor festival and brand partnerships.

将目的地资源转化为连接越野赛事、户外节庆与品牌合作的项目提案。

Project Context

项目背景

Developed destination-specific proposals for Guizhou, Chongqing and Zhoushan, connecting National Geographic storytelling with local landscapes, culture and outdoor participation.

分别为贵州、重庆与舟山开发方案，将国家地理的探索叙事与各地地形、文化和户外参与体验相结合。

Strategic Challenge

战略挑战

The proposal needed to speak to both public-sector destination value and brand-sponsor commercial value, balancing cultural storytelling, event feasibility and measurable impact.

提案需要同时面向政府端目的地价值和品牌赞助商商业价值，平衡文化叙事、赛事可行性和可衡量影响。

Concept Direction

概念方向

The concept reframed a trail race as a “Festival of Wonder”: an event platform where movement becomes a way to explore nature, understand place and create reasons to return.

项目将越野赛事延展为“惊奇生活节”：让运动成为探索自然、理解目的地，并形成重复到访理由的体验平台。

Program Framework

项目框架

  • Destination storytelling and public-sector value
  • Trail race model and race categories
  • Outdoor festival and signature experiences
  • Operations, promotion and measurement planning
  • 目的地叙事与政府端价值
  • 越野赛事模型与组别
  • 户外生活节与标志性体验
  • 运营、传播与测量规划

Destination Storytelling Logic

目的地叙事逻辑

The framework connects local landscape and cultural assets with event participation, content distribution and destination travel, supporting a recurring outdoor event platform.

将各地自然与文化资源连接到赛事参与、内容传播和目的地旅行，形成可持续的户外活动平台。

My Contribution

我的贡献

I developed the destination strategy, race and festival framework, and commercial estimates, connecting local destination assets with participant experiences and sponsor value.

搭建目的地策略、赛事与生活节框架及商业测算，将在地资源转化为参与体验与赞助商价值。

Key Learnings

关键收获

A destination event proposal becomes more credible when cultural meaning, participant experience, operational feasibility and sponsor value are designed as one system.

一个目的地赛事提案，只有把文化意义、参与者体验、运营可行性和品牌合作价值设计为同一套系统，才真正具备说服力。

04 / Community Trail Series Review

04 / 社群越野跑系列复盘

HOKA Trail Running Series

A review of three trail events, assessing community participation and content performance.

通过三站赛事复盘，呈现社群参与及内容传播的表现。

Overview

项目概览

A review of HOKA's Run Wilder events in Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan, analysing event experiences, community participation and brand content performance.

复盘 HOKA「一起野外」虞山、莫干山和王位山三站活动，分析赛事体验、社群参与与品牌内容表现。

My Role

我的角色

I independently developed the full recap, covering data analysis across three events, content performance evaluation, insights, recommendations and report presentation.

独立完成整套复盘，涵盖三站数据分析、内容表现评估、洞察建议与报告呈现。

Challenge

项目挑战

The review needed to identify how three distinct trail environments, creator segments and runner feedback jointly shaped community quality and informed future event decisions.

复盘需识别三种不同赛道环境、达人类型和跑者反馈如何共同影响社群质量，并为后续活动决策提供依据。

Review Approach

复盘策略

I compared registrations, participation, completion, content performance, creator value and runner feedback across the three events to develop content and community recommendations.

从报名、参赛、完赛、内容表现、达人价值和跑者反馈六个维度对比三站，提出内容与社群运营建议。

Execution

项目执行

  • Compared Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan participation data
  • Reviewed WeChat and Xiaohongshu (RED) content performance
  • Mapped creator value across high-reach, high-engagement and KOC groups
  • Synthesised runner feedback, real trail conditions and station-specific highlights
  • 对比虞山、莫干山和王位山三站参与数据
  • 复盘微信与小红书内容表现
  • 分析高覆盖、高互动与 KOC 达人价值
  • 整合跑者反馈、真实赛道体验和各站亮点

Results / Evidence

结果 / 证据

Across the three stations, the series recorded 2,927 registrations, 1,904 participants and 1,720 finishers, for a 90.3% completion rate. Social output included 228 pieces of content, 17,381 interactions and 1.555M cumulative reach.

三站系列累计报名 2,927 人、参赛 1,904 人、完赛 1,720 人，完赛率达 90.3%。社交内容共发布 228 篇，累计互动 17,381 次，累计覆盖 155.5 万。

Key Learnings

关键收获

The recap combined event data with runner experiences to outline five areas for future event planning: route experience, local culture, community moments, content distribution and data review.

结合赛事数据与跑者体验，梳理路线体验、在地文化、社交场景、内容传播和数据复盘五个运营模块，为后续活动提供参考。
